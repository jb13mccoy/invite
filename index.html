Invitation Engine

Fill in an invitation, get a link, and send it. Beside it you get a share image, a print-ready file at 5 × 7, and an animated video.

The whole invitation travels inside the link, in the part after the # that browsers never send to a server. Nothing about your event — a name, an address, a time — is stored anywhere, including here. The card on this page is a live sample.
